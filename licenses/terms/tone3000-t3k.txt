TONE3000 "T3K" terms for the Neural Amp Modeler captures used by Flux's metal guitar rigs
(assets nam-jp-5150-mesa and nam-ob1-5150iii in assets.toml)

As shown on each capture's TONE3000 page when it was downloaded (October 2026):

  "Users may download and use the data file in software and publish the resulting outputs without
  royalties or restrictions. However, they may not upload, republish, or distribute the data file
  without the author's permission."

So: music rendered through the captures may be published, sold and used in games; the .nam files
themselves must never be shipped, uploaded or shared. Flux never bundles them; everyone downloads
them from TONE3000 (free account) and Flux installs them from those downloads.
